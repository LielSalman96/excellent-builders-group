Services

Four things. Done properly.

We keep the list short on purpose. Each of these is a craft, and we would rather be excellent at four than average at ten.

01 · Custom Homes

Ground up, on your lot.

From the first survey stake to the final walkthrough, we build the whole house under one contract with one accountable team. You always know who to call, and the answer is always the same person.

  • Site work, grading and foundations
  • Structural framing and full building envelope
  • Finish carpentry, stone and millwork
  • Smart home, lighting and AV infrastructure

02 · Design + Build

One roof, drawings to keys.

Architecture, engineering and construction working as one team from day one. Nothing gets lost between the drawings and the job site, and budget surprises get caught on paper instead of in concrete.

  • Architectural design and interior detailing
  • Structural engineering coordination
  • City permits and plan check, handled for you
  • A single contract and a single schedule

03 · Remodels & Additions

Respect what stands. Improve the rest.

Second stories, structural remodels and whole home transformations. We open walls carefully, protect what you love about the house, and rebuild the rest to the same standard as our new construction.

  • Whole home remodels and reconfigurations
  • Second story and room additions
  • Kitchens, primary suites and spa baths
  • Structural upgrades and seismic work

04 · Outdoor Living & Pools

The backyard is a room too.

Pools, terraces and outdoor kitchens designed together with the house instead of bolted on after. When the glass walls slide away, inside and outside should feel like one continuous home.

  • Pools, spas and water features
  • Terraces, decks and outdoor kitchens
  • Fire features and landscape lighting
  • Drought smart planting and irrigation
