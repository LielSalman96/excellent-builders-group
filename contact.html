What Happens Next

Three steps, no strings.

  1. The Call

    Ten minutes. You tell us about the property and what you want to build. We tell you if we are the right fit.

  2. The Walk

    We meet on your lot, look at the site together, and give you an honest early range for the work.

  3. The Plan

    If it makes sense for both of us, we move to drawings, a line item budget and a real schedule.

Before You Call

Quick answers.

Is the first consultation free?

The call and the lot walk cost you nothing. Detailed design and engineering work is where fees begin, and we will tell you exactly when that line is coming.

What should I have ready?

Just the address and your ideas. If you already have plans, surveys or inspiration photos, great, bring them. If not, that is what the first conversations are for.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. We work with financing partners and can walk you through the options on the first call, with no obligation.

What areas do you serve?

We are based in Encino and build across Greater Los Angeles, including Sherman Oaks, Calabasas, Bel Air, Beverly Hills, Brentwood and Pacific Palisades.
